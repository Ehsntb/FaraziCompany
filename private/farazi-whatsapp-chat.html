حوزه فعالیت ما

فرازی کمپانی در زمینه تأمین، خرید و فروش، سورسینگ، واردات، صادرات و توزیع انواع نخ و مواد اولیه مورد استفاده در صنعت نساجی فعالیت می‌کند.

یکی از حوزه‌های تخصصی و مهم فعالیت فرازی کمپانی، بیش از ۱۵ سال تجربه تخصصی در واردات و فروش انواع نخ لمه و نخ‌های متالیک در رنگ‌ها، سایزها و مشخصات متنوع است.

سبد محصولات و فعالیت‌های تجاری ما شامل موارد زیر است:

• نخ لمه و نخ‌های متالیک در رنگ‌ها و سایزهای مختلف
• نخ پنبه
• نخ اسپان
• نخ فلامنت
• نخ پلی‌استر
• نخ ویسکوز
• انواع نخ‌های مورد استفاده در بافندگی و تولید پارچه
• سایر مواد اولیه مورد نیاز صنعت نساجی

ما با تکیه بر بیش از چهار دهه تجربه و فعالیت مستمر در بازار و ۱۵ سال تجربه تخصصی در حوزه نخ‌های لمه و متالیک، همواره تلاش می‌کنیم با شناسایی منابع معتبر و ایجاد ارتباط مؤثر با تأمین‌کنندگان داخلی و بین‌المللی، نیاز مشتریان و شرکای تجاری خود را به بهترین شکل تأمین کنیم.

Farazi Company – Company Profile

Over 40 Years of Experience in Textile Yarn Trading

15 Years of Specialized Experience in Lurex & Metallic Yarn Import and Trading

With over 40 years of experience in the textile market and yarn trading industry, and more than 15 years of specialized experience in importing and trading Lurex and metallic yarns in a wide range of colors and sizes, Farazi Company specializes in the sourcing, supply, trading, import, export, and distribution of textile yarns and raw materials.

Based in the Tehran Grand Bazaar, Iran, Farazi Company has built its experience and commercial relationships through decades of continuous activity in the textile market.

We work closely with manufacturers, textile factories, weaving workshops, fabric producers, wholesalers, garment manufacturers, traders, and domestic and international business partners.

With more than four decades of market experience, combined with 15 years of specialized expertise in Lurex and metallic yarns, we focus on providing reliable sourcing, consistent supply, suitable products, and competitive commercial solutions for our customers and business partners.

────────

Our Products & Business Activities

Farazi Company is active in the sourcing, supply, trading, import, export, and distribution of a wide range of textile yarns and raw materials.

One of our key areas of specialization is the import and trading of Lurex and metallic yarns, backed by more than 15 years of specialized experience and offering a wide range of colors and sizes.

Our products and business activities include:

• Lurex & Metallic Yarn in various colors and sizes
• Cotton Yarn
• Spun Yarn
• Filament Yarn
• Polyester Yarn
• Viscose Yarn
• Various yarns used in weaving and textile production
• Other textile raw materials

With over 40 years of experience and continuous activity in the market, as well as 15 years of specialized experience in Lurex and metallic yarns, we work to identify reliable sources and establish effective relationships with domestic and international suppliers.

Our Market Experience & Expertise

With more than four decades of experience in the textile market, Farazi Company has developed a strong understanding of market requirements, customer needs, sourcing processes, and the trading of textile yarns and raw materials.

In addition, our 15 years of specialized experience in importing and trading Lurex and metallic yarns has enabled us to develop strong expertise in this specific sector of the textile industry.

Our presence in the Tehran Grand Bazaar, one of Iran’s major commercial and trading centers, enables us to maintain close relationships with textile traders, manufacturers, weavers, wholesalers, suppliers, and buyers.

By closely following market developments and understanding customer requirements, we aim to provide efficient, reliable, and professional solutions for sourcing and trading textile yarns and raw materials.
