About Farazi Company

Supply, Import, Export & Trading of Textile Raw Materials

With over 40 years of experience in the textile market and yarn trading industry, and more than 15 years of specialized experience in importing and trading Lurex and metallic yarns in a wide range of colors and sizes, Farazi Company specializes in the sourcing, supply, trading, import, export, and distribution of textile yarns and raw materials.

Based in the Tehran Grand Bazaar, Iran, Farazi Company has built its experience and commercial relationships through decades of continuous activity in the textile market.

We work closely with manufacturers, textile factories, weaving workshops, fabric producers, wholesalers, garment manufacturers, traders, and domestic and international business partners. With more than four decades of market experience, combined with 15 years of specialized expertise in Lurex and metallic yarns, we focus on providing reliable sourcing, consistent supply, suitable products, and competitive commercial solutions for our customers and business partners.

Choosing the right yarn influences the appearance, texture and quality of the finished textile. End use, yarn count, fibre composition, colour and required quantity all matter. We aim to begin with your actual business needs, whether you are selecting yarn for a specific product or exploring sourcing options for your operation.

Our field of work

From yarn selection to sourcing

Our products and business activities include: Lurex & Metallic Yarn in various colors and sizes, Cotton Yarn, Spun Yarn, Filament Yarn, Polyester Yarn, Viscose Yarn, Various yarns used in weaving and textile production, Other textile raw materials.

Our focus on Lurex and metallic yarns draws on an understanding of their colours, types, counts and thicknesses. For manufacturers who value both the visual character of a fabric and its suitability for its intended use, reviewing these specifications is an important part of the buying decision. Alongside this specialism, our broader yarn range supports discussions around different weaving and fabric-production requirements.

If you already know the yarn you need, share its type, count, colour and required quantity with us. If you are still comparing options, tell us about the intended application and the characteristics you want to achieve. Availability, current pricing and sourcing terms are discussed directly for each enquiry.
