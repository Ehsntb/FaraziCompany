About Farazi Company

Four decades alongside the textile industry

Our story is rooted in Tehran’s Grand Bazaar. With over 40 years of experience, Farazi Company works in the specialized supply of textile raw materials and connects the needs of the textile industry with international sourcing.

From selecting the right yarn to discussing sourcing requirements, we believe that understanding the material and listening to our customers are the foundations of a lasting partnership.
